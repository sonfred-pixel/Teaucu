TEAUCU FAMILY SHOWCASE

LIGHT BEARERS

The Light Bearers family exists to shine the light of Christ in every corner of campus life. Rooted in Matthew 5:14, this family is known for its vibrant fellowship, deep devotion to prayer, and commitment to walking in the truth. With a welcoming spirit and strong mentorship, Light Bearers nurtures disciples who influence others with the love and wisdom of God.

ST STEPHENS

Named after the first Christian martyr, the St. Stephens family stands out for its boldness in faith, compassion, and service. Members are encouraged to live sacrificially and speak truth in love, modeling the spirit of Stephen. This family fosters honest conversations, deep Bible study, and a strong sense of accountability, making it a solid foundation for spiritual growth

THE EAGLES

With Isaiah 40:31 as their anchor, The Eagles family soars with vision, strength, and purpose. Known for their energy, unity, and commitment to excellence, The Eagles challenge each other to rise above the ordinary and live for God’s glory. Leadership is nurtured, creativity is embraced, and faith is stirred in this dynamic and uplifting family.

PILGRIMS OF CHRIST

Pilgrims of Christ is a family that journeys together with eyes fixed on eternity. Reflecting the pilgrim’s heart, this group is marked by humility, endurance, and a hunger for God. Whether through fellowship, worship nights, or encouraging each other in struggles, this family walks hand-in-hand, growing in Christ and preparing for the home that awaits above.
