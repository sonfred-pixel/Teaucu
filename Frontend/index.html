Winning Souls for Christ

SINCE 2016

TEAUCU (The East African University Christian Union) is a student-led organization focused on spiritual growth, leadership, and community service. We empower students to develop their faith, talents, and leadership skills, making a positive impact on campus and beyond.

VISION

A transformed generation ready to serve Christ

MISSION

Winning souls for Christ through our actions and service to God and human, transforming lives and equipping them with the word of God.
