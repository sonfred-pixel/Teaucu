Where Does My Contribution Go?

DEVELOPMENT AND MAINTENANCE

It's essential to ensure that our facilities are well-maintained and equipped to serve our congregation and community effectively. This includes utilities, repairs, upgrades, and ongoing operational expenses necessary for providing a conducive environment for worship, fellowship, and outreach activities.

OUTREACH AND MISSION ACTIVITIES

A significant portion of the offerings and tithes goes towards outreach and mission activities including local and national mission trips, evangelism efforts, community outreach programs and support for missionaries and organizations dedicated to spreading the Gospel. We prioritize projects that aim to meet the spiritual, physical, and emotional needs of individuals and communities.

MISCELLANEOUS EXPENSES

Beyond the outlined categories, there are miscellaneous expenses associated with running a ministry effectively. We strive to manage these expenses efficiently to maximize the impact of your contributions on fulfilling our mission and serving others.
