Our Ministries

Discover the various ways we serve and worship together at TEAUCU

Servicing God Through Many Gifts

At TEAUCU, we believe that every member has unique gifts to contribute to God's kingdom. Our ministries provide opportunities for service, growth, and fellowship. Explore the various ways you can get involved and use your talents for God's glory.

PRAISE AND WORSHIP

The Praise and Worship Department leads the congregation into God's presence through powerful, Spirit-led worship. With hearts tuned to heaven and lives surrendered in service, they create an atmosphere where believers can freely express adoration to God.

CHOIR

The Choir is the voice of harmony and unity in TEAUCU. Through songs of praise, hymns, and anthems, they proclaim the gospel with power and beauty. The Choir also serves as a close knit community of growth, discipline, and spiritual encouragement.

TECHNICALS

This behind the scenes powerhouse ensures every worship experience is well supported and spirit filled. The Technicals Department is made up of skilled instrumentalists and sound operators who serve faithfully, blending talent and anointing to enhance worship.

COMMUNICATIONS/MEDIA

Tasked with telling the CU's story and spreading the gospel online and offline, the Communications Department is a creative hub for design, media, and messaging. They serve with clarity and excellence ensuring that every message reflects God's truth and love.

HOSPITALITY & USHERING

The Hospitality and Ushering Department is the welcoming face of the CU. With warmth, order, and joy, they make every fellowship feel like home. They embody servant-heartedness and create a space where everyone feels seen and valued.

MISSIONS AND TRIPS

This department carries the heartbeat of the Great Commission. Through mission outreaches, community visits, and organized trips, they extend Christ's love beyond campus. They stir up a passion for service and expose members to real world ministry.

EVANGELISM/DISCIPLESHIP

At the core of the CU's spiritual mission, this department equips members to share the gospel and walk alongside others in faith. They organize evangelistic drives, one-on-one mentorships, and discipleship programs.

BIBLE STUDY

The Bible Study Department is where truth is explored, faith is deepened, and transformation begins. Through weekly studies, teaching sessions, and small group discussions, they help members understand Scripture and apply it in daily life.

PRAYER AND INTERCESSION

The Prayer and Intercessions Team is the spiritual engine of the Christian Union. Committed to standing in the gap, they labor in prayer for the CU, the campus, and the world beyond. They believe that revival begins on the knees.
